2. The Service

RunVue turns the runs recorded in Health Connect (or GPX/FIT files you import) into 3D replay videos made entirely on your device, from a following third-person view and a first-person highlight view.

  • No accounts: the App has no user accounts. Your imported runs and videos are kept on your device. We do not operate a server for your data; the App only downloads map data from our static storage.
  • Read-only health access: the App reads workouts, routes, heart rate, speed, distance, and (optionally) calories from Health Connect with your permission, and never writes to Health Connect.
  • Requirements: the App requires Android 9 or later, Health Connect, and an internet connection to download map data for new areas. Video quality, rendering time, and available features may depend on your device.
  • Map coverage: map data comes from open data and may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate in some areas. Some areas may not be available.
