Giggly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Giggly ("the App", "we", "our") is developed and operated by JUKI, an individual developer in the Republic of Korea (business registration number 604-20-14584). Contact: support@setlog.net.

Giggly is a comic camera app for iPhone and Android. Point the camera at a face and red strings pop up above the head, with a number of strings worked out from face shape; in Pets mode it does the same for cats and dogs. This Privacy Policy covers both the iPhone and the Android app. The App has no user accounts, and we do not run a server that receives your data. The only online service we operate is a small regional check described in Section 7, which stores nothing.

In short: faces are processed on your device. Face shape is held only in temporary memory while a face is on screen, is never stored, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clips stay on your device unless you save or share them. The App does not ask for your name, email, contacts, or precise location. To keep the App free, it uses Google services for ads (AdMob), app statistics and crash reports (Firebase); they receive device and usage information described in Sections 8 and 9, which does not include face data, numbers, or your age. Ads are not personalized, and the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The App does not ask for your age; Giggly is rated for ages 13 and up.

2. Camera and Face Processing

When you use People mode, the App analyzes camera frames on your device with Google's open-source MediaPipe library to find face landmark points (such as the eyes, nose, mouth, and outline) and head angle. From the shape of a face it calculates the number of strings by a fixed formula, and it uses expressions only to change the animation. The App does not recognize or identify who anyone is, does not compare faces with stored faces, and does not estimate or show age, gender, or attractiveness.

  • On your device only: camera frames, face landmarks, measurements, and the resulting numbers are processed in the App's temporary memory. They are not written to storage, not included in backups, and not sent to us, to Google, to advertisers, or to anyone else. They are not included in app statistics or crash reports.
  • Discarded quickly: the information for a face is discarded within about half a second after that face leaves the view, and when the camera stops, you switch mode or camera, an ad is shown, the App goes to the background, or the App closes. If the same person comes back, the App calculates the number again from scratch; it does not remember them.
  • Other people in the picture: the App processes every face in view in the same way. Please only point the camera at people who are happy to join in.
  • Pets mode: face detection for people is off; the App detects cats and dogs as boxes in the picture, on your device.
  • Consent: before the camera is first used, the App explains this processing, its use of Google services for ads, statistics, and crash reports, and the rolling video buffer (Section 5), with links to this policy and the Biometric Policy, and asks you to tap Agree & Continue. Ads, statistics, and crash reporting start only after you agree; then the system asks for camera permission. You can turn camera access off at any time in your device settings.

Some laws treat face geometry as biometric data. How long face geometry is kept and how it is destroyed is set out in our Biometric Data Retention and Destruction Policy, which is part of this Privacy Policy.

3. Age and App Store Age Signals

  • The App does not ask for your age or birth date. Giggly is rated for ages 13 and up on the App Store and Google Play, so parental controls set up on a device can block it.
  • App store age signals: where Apple or Google provide them, the App asks your app store for an age range (Apple's Declared Age Range on iPhone; Google Play's Age Signals on Android). The answer is used on your device only. Your app store's own privacy policy applies to how it handles your age information.
  • If the app store indicates the user is under 13, the App stops: it shows a "Giggly is for ages 13+" screen and does not use the camera or start ads, app statistics, crash reports, or the regional check. If this signal arrives after those services have started, the App stops them and clears the app statistics and unsent crash reports held on the device.
  • If the app store reports that a parent's approval is pending or was declined (for example under Texas law), the App waits or stops until it is approved.
  • In all other cases — including when no signal is available — the App works the same way, and the ad and statistics settings in Sections 8 and 9 are the same for every user, whatever their age.
  • What is saved: the resulting state on your device — allowed (teen, adult, or unconfirmed), blocked, waiting for a parent, or declined. This means a broad age category (teen or adult) can be stored, but never your birth date or exact age, and it is never sent to us. So that a block cannot be bypassed by reinstalling, on iPhone it is kept in the Keychain on this device (not synced to iCloud Keychain), stays after the App is deleted, and may be included in device backups and restored by a device transfer; on Android it is the one App file stored in app data and included in Android backup and device transfer.
  • Your age range or this state is never sent to us, to analytics, to crash reports, or to advertisers.

4. "My Number" (optional)

You can choose to pin your own number on your phone, so that your solo reveals always land on it. If you agree on the confirmation screen, the App saves only which of the 11 possible numbers you got (one value) and the date you set it — not your face, face points, measurements, or images. It is stored only on your device (on iPhone in the Keychain, set to stay on this device and not move to backups; on Android excluded from backup and transfer), and it is never sent to us or used for ads or analytics. You can delete it at any time in Settings → My Number → Delete; reinstalling the App also removes it. App statistics record only that a number was set or deleted, never the number.

5. Clips, Microphone, Photos, and Sharing

  • Rolling buffer: while the camera is open, the App keeps the last few seconds (up to about 10) of camera video in temporary memory on your device so that you can clip a moment after it happens. It is never written to storage or sent anywhere. If you allow the microphone, the last few seconds of audio are kept the same way, only while the camera screen is open and active. Older seconds are overwritten continuously, and the buffer is cleared when an ad is shown, you switch camera or mode, or the App goes to the background. Nothing is kept unless you make a clip.
  • Making a clip: when you tap to clip, the App creates the video on your device and shows a preview. The working files are stored in the App's temporary folder (excluded from backups) and deleted when you close the preview; any left over, for example after a crash, are deleted the next time the App starts.
  • Saving: a clip is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save. On iPhone the App asks only for permission to add photos — it cannot see your library. On Android it saves through the system media store without reading your other photos.
  • Sharing: when you share, the clip is handed to the app you choose through the iPhone share sheet or the Android share menu (or, if direct TikTok sharing is available in your version, to TikTok; on iPhone this saves the clip to your photo library first). From then on, that app's privacy policy applies. We never receive your clips.
  • The microphone and photo permissions are optional. You can change them at any time in your device settings.

6. What the App Does Not Ask For

The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise (GPS) location. It does not read your photo library. If you email us, we receive what you send (Section 12).

7. Regional Availability Check

People mode is not available in some areas. To decide this, the App first checks on your device whether the device appears to be set up for the United States and is set to a U.S. Central time zone. Only if both are true does it contact our regional check service, and again only when the saved result expires. Devices that do not match are not checked.

  • The request contains no account, device, or advertising identifier and no cookies. The service, which runs on Cloudflare, replies only with the country and region that Cloudflare associates with the request's IP address.
  • To check whether a feature is available in your area, Giggly briefly uses your IP address through Cloudflare (acting as our service provider). Giggly does not store your IP address or region, and does not send them to analytics. Cloudflare may process the IP address briefly for security purposes, such as protecting the service from abuse.
  • The App stores the result on your device (for up to 7 days if People mode is unavailable, otherwise up to 24 hours, and less if your time zone or country changes) so it does not need to ask again every time.
  • If People mode is unavailable, Pets mode and the rest of the App still work.

8. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows full-screen and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. A full-screen ad can appear only after you tap Close on a clip preview, once the preview has closed — never during a scan, reveal, or recording, and never as you open or return to the App. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one.

  • Non-personalized ads for everyone: the App asks Google for non-personalized ads only, for every user, and limits ad content to a PG rating. The App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), does not access the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not combine your information with other companies' data to target ads.
  • What Google still receives: even for non-personalized ads, Google may collect and process, under its own policies, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, and app version), identifiers used for frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention (on Android, the advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and information about the ads shown and your interaction with them. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and non-personalized ads.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: all ad requests are marked as coming from users under the age of consent, so Google serves only limited ads and no consent form is shown.
  • The App never sends face data, numbers, titles, expressions, Crush Dex contents, your age range, or whether you are in Korea to advertisers, and does not use them to target ads. We set AdMob to block dating, gambling, weight-loss, cosmetic-procedure, and sexually suggestive ad categories.

Your choices: on Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's privacy or ads settings. Where Google's privacy tools require it (for example in certain U.S. states), the App shows a Privacy choices row in its Settings where you can review or change your choices.

9. App Statistics, Crash Reports, and Remote Settings (Google Firebase)

The App starts Google Firebase only after you tap Agree & Continue on the first-launch screen (and right away on later launches) — never before it, and never for a user the app store indicates is under 13 or who is waiting for a parent's approval:

  • Firebase Analytics (app statistics): a fixed list of events about how the App is used, such as onboarding steps, a reveal completed (People or Pets mode, front or back camera), a clip saved or shared, ad shown or skipped, rewarded-ad results, a string style chosen, My Number set or deleted (without the number), microphone and photo permission results, and performance level. Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from the IP address. Events never include face data, numbers, titles, expressions, your age range, or the regional check result. We do not set a user ID, and advertising-identifier collection by Analytics is turned off.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): when the App crashes or hits a serious error, a report with the technical details (stack trace, device model, operating system, app version, memory and storage state, a crash-reporting installation identifier, and a few fixed technical labels such as the app screen category and performance level).
  • Firebase Remote Config: the App downloads settings (for example ad frequency limits and text variations). This request includes a Firebase installation identifier and app information.

Google consent signals: for every user, "ad user data" and "ad personalization" are always set to "denied". In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, "ad storage" and "analytics storage" are also set to "denied".

Minimal collection on devices set up for Korea: if your device appears to be set up for Korea (device region, SIM or mobile network country, Korean time zone, or the Korean App Store), the App turns Firebase Analytics off, so no app usage events are sent. Ads stay non-personalized as above, and crash reports and remote settings still work. This check happens on your device; the result is not stored or sent anywhere.

We use this information only to understand how the App is used, fix problems, and tune the App; it is not linked to your identity. See Firebase privacy and security.

11. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not give face data to anyone. The App works with:

  • Google LLC — AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and consent), Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), and on Android, Google Play's Age Signals (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple — on iPhone, the App Store and Declared Age Range (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — runs the regional check (Section 7) and forwards email sent to support@setlog.net to the developer's mailbox (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • Apps you share to — receive the clips you choose to share

These providers receive only the information described in this Privacy Policy, for the purposes described here, and they are bound by their terms and data protection commitments to provide the same or equal protection of that information as this Privacy Policy describes.

We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell your personal information for money, and the App does not use personalized or cross-context behavioral advertising for anyone. Under some U.S. state laws (for example California), device information and identifiers received by Google's ad services may still count as "sharing" or "selling"; where Google's U.S. state message applies, you can opt out through Privacy choices in the App's Settings (Section 8).

12. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, U.S. state privacy laws, or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

  • Most information stays on your device and is under your control: delete My Number in Settings, change permissions in your device settings, change ad choices as in Section 8, or delete the App.
  • Information held by Google (ads, Firebase) is linked to random identifiers, not to your name. Because the App has no accounts, we may not be able to find information about you, but we will help where we can — email us at support@setlog.net.
  • If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, and delete them within 90 days after your inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

  • Face processing on your device and My Number: your explicit consent, given on the in-app screens. You can withdraw it by turning off camera access or deleting My Number.
  • Limited, non-personalized ads (with ad storage turned off) and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the free App. App statistics (with analytics storage turned off): our legitimate interest in understanding how the App is used.
  • Crash reports, remote settings, app store age signals, and the regional check: our legitimate interests in keeping the App working, secure, and compliant with local law.

13. Retention and Deletion

  • Face geometry: only while a face is on screen (see the Biometric Data Retention and Destruction Policy).
  • Rolling video and audio buffer: a few seconds (up to about 10), in memory, while the camera is open; clip working files: until the preview closes or the next launch.
  • On-device items (Section 10): until you delete them or the App, except as noted for the age signal state.
  • Firebase: crash reports are kept for 90 days; Analytics event-level data is kept for 2 months, after which only aggregated reports remain.
  • AdMob, Google Play, and Apple: according to their own policies.
  • Regional check: nothing is stored by Giggly; Cloudflare security processing is short-term.
  • Emails you send us: deleted within 90 days after your inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires longer.

14. Children and Teens

Giggly is rated 13+ and is not intended for children under 13. The App does not ask users for their age. If the app store tells the App that a user is under 13, the App shows a "Giggly is for ages 13+" screen and cannot be used; it does not start the camera, analytics, crash reporting, ads, or the regional check for that user (Section 3). If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and personal information was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

The same protections apply to every user, including teens: non-personalized ads only, a PG ad rating, no tracking request or IDFA access, and Google's "ad user data" and "ad personalization" signals set to "denied" (Sections 8 and 9).

Korea: Giggly is not intended for children under 14 in Korea. On devices set up for Korea, the App turns off app statistics (Section 9).

15. International Transfers

We are based in the Republic of Korea. Google and Cloudflare may process information in the United States and other countries where they or their partners operate. Where required, these transfers are protected by the providers' safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

16. Security

Face processing happens inside the App on your device. Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). On-device files are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read; My Number is kept in the iPhone Keychain or in Android app storage excluded from backup. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit what exists in the first place.
