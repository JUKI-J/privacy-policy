IntervalRunner Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

IntervalRunner ("the App", "we", "our") is developed and operated by the developer reachable at setlog.net

IntervalRunner is an interval running app for iPhone and Apple Watch. You set time, distance, or heart-rate intervals and the App alerts you by voice, sound, and haptics when you drift from your target pace. The App has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no server of ours. This Privacy Policy explains what the App accesses on your device, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

In short: the developer does not collect your data. Your health, location, and workout data stay on your device and, if you allow it, are written to the Health app. It is never sent to us, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.

Data the App Uses

Everything below is processed on your device only. Each permission is requested when it is first needed, and you can change it at any time in Settings.

Health Data (HealthKit)

  • Written to Health (with your permission): workouts (outdoor or indoor running, including the interval segments), distance, active energy, the workout route, and heart-rate readings recorded during a workout — including those from a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor you connect to your iPhone.
  • Read from Health (with your permission): heart rate, resting heart rate, date of birth, body weight, and running speed.
  • Why: to record your run in the Health app, to measure heart rate during a run, and to suggest heart-rate zones and estimate calories. The App does not request or read any other Health data.
  • Where it goes: HealthKit data is kept on your device and in your Health database. The App never uploads it to any server and does not store it in iCloud. How the Health app itself stores and syncs your data (for example, through iCloud) is managed by Apple and your own settings.
  • How it is not used: HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, is never sold, and is never disclosed to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. It is not used to promote the subscription.
  • Optional: if you deny read access, the App still works; heart-rate zones and calories that depend on that data are simply not filled in automatically. You can review or revoke access in the Health app (Sharing → Apps) or in Settings.

Location ("While Using the App" only)

  • What: your position during an outdoor workout.
  • Why: to calculate your pace and distance and to record your route. It keeps working with the screen off while a workout is active, and iOS shows its usual location indicator.
  • Limits: the App never asks for "Always" location access and does not use location when no workout is running. Treadmill mode does not use location at all.
  • Where it goes: stored with the workout on your device and, if Health access is granted, in the workout route in Health. It is not sent to us. Route and coordinates leave the device only when you export a GPX file or share an image yourself; the shared image does not include a map unless you switch it on; when it does, the route's first and last 300 m are left out and the map is blurred within about 300 m of the start and the finish.

Motion & Fitness

  • What: step count and cadence from the device's motion sensors.
  • Why: to refine GPS pace, detect stopping and starting, and show a reference pace on the treadmill.

Bluetooth

  • What: a connection to a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor you choose.
  • Why: to read your heart rate during a run. The App does not scan for or connect to anything else and does not use Bluetooth for tracking.

Notifications

  • What: local notifications scheduled on your device, for example a reminder before a free trial ends or when a workout has been paused for a long time. There are no push notifications and no marketing messages.

Voice Guidance

  • Spoken alerts are pre-made voice clips (AI-generated speech) bundled with the App and played on your device. No speech is generated or downloaded while you use the App. The App does not use the microphone or record audio.

What We Do Not Collect

IntervalRunner does not collect, store on any server, or share any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier — the App has no accounts or sign-in
  • Your health, fitness, or location data
  • Contacts, photos, or browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data
  • Payment information — purchases are handled entirely by Apple
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports sent automatically

The App uses no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases (Subscriptions)

IntervalRunner Pro is sold as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store. Purchases, free trials, billing, renewals, refunds, and Family Sharing are handled entirely by Apple using StoreKit. We never see or store your Apple Account details or payment information. The App reads your subscription status from Apple on your device to unlock Pro features and keeps a small cached copy of that status on the device so Pro also works offline (see Data Stored on Your Device). You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following stay on your device and are never uploaded to any server:

  • Your saved workouts, presets, and settings
  • Workout history: summaries, step-by-step results, heart-rate and pace samples, and routes
  • Local counters used to improve the App's design, such as how many times a screen was shown; they are never sent anywhere automatically (see Diagnostics below)
  • Engine traces of your recent workouts, used only for the diagnostics file: speeds, distances, heart rate, step states, and alert events, with positions only in a private coordinate frame (see Diagnostics below); and a temporary recovery point for a workout in progress
  • A cached copy of your subscription status: whether Pro is active, the kind of subscription (trial, monthly, or yearly), when it expires, and when it was last checked with Apple. It contains no payment or Apple Account details. It is kept in the App's own storage and in the iOS Keychain; iOS may keep Keychain items even after the App is uninstalled

If you use Apple Watch, the App exchanges workouts, settings, and live workout state between your iPhone and your own paired Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity and HealthKit. This traffic goes directly between your devices. The App's widgets and Live Activities read a small amount of the same data (for example the current step and remaining time) on your device.

IntervalRunner does not use iCloud or CloudKit to sync data. As with other app data, these files may be included in the iCloud or computer backups you make of your device, which are governed by Apple's terms and your own backup settings.

Diagnostics (Only If You Choose)

If something goes wrong, you can export a diagnostics file from Settings → Data & Privacy. Nothing is sent automatically. The file is created on your device and leaves it only if you pick a destination in the share sheet (for example, emailing it to us).

  • What it contains: device model, iOS version, app version, language and region setting, time zone, Low Power Mode state, the status of each permission, audio output history (output types such as speaker or headphones, never device names), the local counters described above, and the engine traces of your most recent workouts on the iPhone, together with any traces your Watch has sent to it (speeds, distances, heart rate, step states, and alert events). A file exported from the App's hidden developer tools may also contain the App's own log lines from the last 24 hours, with coordinate-like text removed.
  • What it does not contain: no latitude or longitude. Positions in traces are converted to a private coordinate frame with a random origin and rotation for each session, so they cannot be mapped to a real place, and any trace that contains coordinate fields is left out. No name, email, workout history, or Health data values other than the heart-rate figures in the traces.
  • Crash reports: the App has no crash-reporting SDK. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash information through Xcode Organizer and App Store Connect; we do not receive anything that identifies you.

Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties, and the developer receives none of it. The only services involved are Apple's own: the App Store and StoreKit for purchases, and HealthKit and the Health app for the data you choose to record. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Files you export or share yourself (GPX, CSV, JSON, images) go wherever you send them.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

  • Permissions: you can review or change Location, Health, Motion & Fitness, Notifications, and Bluetooth at any time in the iOS Settings app; Settings → Data & Privacy in the App shows their current status and links there.
  • Delete a workout or run: from the history screens in the App.
  • Delete all data: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data removes the App's workouts, history, settings, alert profiles, traces, diagnostics files, temporary export files, and widget data on the iPhone. It also clears the Keychain copy of your subscription status; the App then re-reads the status from Apple, so an active subscription stays active. It tells the paired Watch to do the same, and the Watch applies this the next time the Watch app runs; workouts recorded on the Watch after you deleted are kept. Health data is handled separately: the delete dialog has its own "Also delete from Health" switch (off by default) that removes the workouts the App saved to Health, and it only deletes the ones it created. Everything else in Health remains under your control in the Health app.
  • Uninstall: removing the App removes its data from that device, except the cached subscription status in the iOS Keychain, which iOS may keep and which the App reuses if you install it again (use Delete All Data before uninstalling to clear it). Workouts already saved to Health stay in Health until you delete them there.
  • Subscription: the subscription itself is managed by Apple; the App keeps only the cached status described above.

Children's Privacy

IntervalRunner does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information, there is nothing to collect from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
