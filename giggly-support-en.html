Giggly Support

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Need help? Email support@setlog.net. Please include your phone model, its system version, and the App version (Settings → bottom of the screen). We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my number real?

No. Giggly is for fun only — numbers aren't real. The number and title are a playful effect worked out from face shape by a fixed formula. They say nothing about how many people like you, and nothing about looks, age, or anything else about a person.

Does Giggly save or send my face?

No. Faces are processed on your phone only, in temporary memory while a face is on screen. Face shape is never stored and never sent to us or anyone else. See the Biometric Data Retention and Destruction Policy.

What does the camera keep while it's open?

While the camera is open, the last few seconds of video (and of sound, if you turned the microphone on) stay in your phone's memory so you can clip a moment after it happens. Older seconds are overwritten. Nothing is saved unless you make a clip and choose to save or share it.

Why do I see ads? Are they personalized?

Ads keep Giggly free. They are non-personalized for everyone, and the App doesn't ask to track you. A full-screen ad can appear only after you close a clip preview (and, if we switch it on, when you go back from the Crush Dex to the camera) — never during a scan or recording. If you would rather not see them, there is an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase (see below).

What does Remove Ads include?

It is one optional, one-time purchase (not a subscription) in Settings → Remove Ads. It removes pop-up ads and unlocks string styles, clip effects, and pet effects without watching ads. Your numbers stay the same, and clips still carry the Giggly watermark. The price is shown by your app store in your local currency. Family sharing is not supported.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with, then tap Settings → Restore Purchases. It can also restore by itself after a reinstall. If it says it found nothing, check that you are signed in to the right store account; a purchase made with a different account cannot be restored. If you still need help, email us and tell us your phone model and which store you bought from (please do not send card numbers).

It says "Waiting for approval". What now?

Your account needs a parent or family manager to approve the purchase (for example Ask to Buy on iPhone, or Family Link on Android). Once it is approved, ads disappear and everything unlocks on its own — you may need to open the App once. On iPhone you can tap Ask again if the request expired. Approval requests can expire (Apple's last 24 hours, and Google refunds Android purchases that are not completed within 3 days), so please approve soon after the request is sent.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us, and we cannot issue them directly. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history (or Order history) and choose the purchase. After a refund, ads come back the next time you start the App. If you are in Korea, see the withdrawal information in the Terms of Use.

I can't open the App any more but I paid for Remove Ads. Can I get a refund?

If the App was blocked for you after you bought Remove Ads (for example because your app store later indicated you are under 13), Restore is not possible because the App cannot be used. Please request a refund from the store as described above (iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com; Android: Google Play order history). If the store declines, email us at support@setlog.net with your store and the date of purchase and we will try to help.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Tap Settings → Report an ad. An email to us opens with a short template — tell us what the ad showed, about when you saw it, and where (you can attach a screenshot). It does not include your device or advertising ID. We review reports and block the ad in our ad settings where we can.

How do I delete my data?

There's no account, and we don't keep your data on a server. To delete your pinned number, go to Settings → My Number → Delete. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your phone (on iPhone, a small record of the app store age-signal outcome — not your age — may remain in the Keychain; see the Privacy Policy). Clips you saved stay in your photo library until you delete them there.

People mode isn't available where I am. Why?

People mode is turned off in some areas because of local rules. Pets mode and the rest of the App still work.

Who can use Giggly?

Giggly is for ages 13 and up (14 and up in Korea). Only point the camera at people who are happy to join in, and ask before you post a clip of someone.
