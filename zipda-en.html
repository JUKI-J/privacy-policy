Zipda — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [release date]

Zipda does not collect any personal data.

No collection, no tracking

  • All archive (compress/extract) operations happen entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server.
  • No accounts, no advertising, and no analytics SDKs of any kind.

Network activity

Zipda makes no network requests of its own. The app functions fully offline.

Payments

Purchases, if any, are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. Zipda never accesses or stores your payment information.

Third-party sharing

Because Zipda collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to any third party.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date.
